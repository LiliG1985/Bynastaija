BY NASTASIJA WEBSITE

OPEN-ME-website-preview.html
  The whole website in one file, with every photo and logo built in.
  Double-click it to view all pages in your browser.

website-upload-folder/
  The same website split into code files. Upload this whole folder
  to a web host (Vercel, Netlify, or your domain provider).
    index.html   page structure and wording
    styles.css   design: colours, fonts, layout
    script.js    booking calendar, deposit step, forms, prices
    img/         all photos and logos (keep next to index.html)

TO ADD NASTASIJA'S DETAILS
  Open script.js and edit the CONFIG section at the top:
    whatsapp      her number, digits only, e.g. 971501234567
    instagram     her handle
    email         her email address
    openDays, open, close, consultTimes   her hours
    deposits      deposit amounts in AED
    paymentLinks  paste Stripe (or similar) payment links here.
                  Until these are added, the deposit step runs in
                  preview mode and no card is charged.
