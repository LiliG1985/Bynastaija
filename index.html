About

Hi, I’m Nastasija

My studio is small on purpose: one client at a time, no rushing, and a plan built for your skin rather than a menu.

Most people come to me for one of two things. Some want their skin to behave: less pigmentation, fewer breakouts, smoother texture, that glow from within. Others want to stop filling in their brows and lips every morning. Often it’s both.

For skin, I work with professional ranges including PHformula and Circadia, and I use Dermapen 4 for microneedling, paired with Hyla Active, salmon and peptides, pure exosomes, stem cells or BioRePeel depending on what your skin needs.

For permanent makeup, I design lip blush and brows around your face, colouring and lifestyle. The aim is always soft and natural: people notice you look well, not that you’ve had something done.
