BY NASTASIJA · BOOKING BACKEND SETUP
=====================================
Time needed: about 15 minutes. Free. Everything runs on Nastasija's Google account.

WHAT YOU GET
- A private STUDIO DASHBOARD on the website (bottom of every page: "Studio login",
  or go to the website address followed by #admin). Nastasija logs in with her own
  password, on her phone or computer, and sees:
    Diary          today and upcoming appointments, consultation calls and blocked time
    Bookings       every booking, search and filter by status
    Clients        every client with contact details, private notes and full treatment history
    Consultations  free consultation calls
    Messages       contact form messages
    Block time     take time off (holidays, training) so it can't be booked
  Tapping a booking lets her change the status (Deposit paid, Completed, Cancelled, No show),
  send the deposit message on WhatsApp, or open the client's profile.
  Everything below happens automatically behind the dashboard.
- A Google Sheet called "By Nastasija · Bookings & Clients" with 4 tabs:
    Bookings       every treatment booking, its status and a "Send on WhatsApp" link
    Consultations  every free consultation call
    Clients        one row per client, with their full treatment history
    Messages       contact form messages
- Every booking appears in her Google Calendar with the client's phone, email,
  notes and past treatments in the description.
    Yellow = reserved, waiting for deposit
    Green  = deposit paid (confirmed)
    Grey   = done
    Light blue = consultation call
- An email to skinbynastasija@gmail.com for every new booking, with a button that
  opens WhatsApp with the "please pay your 50% deposit" message already written
  (including the bank details) for that client. One tap to send.
- An automatic confirmation email to the client with the bank details (if they gave an email).
- The website calendar only shows free times: it reads her Google Calendar, leaves
  10 minutes between clients and keeps 13:00 to 14:00 free for lunch.
  Anything she adds to her calendar herself (holidays, personal plans) also blocks those times.


STEP 1 · Create the script
1. Sign in to Google as skinbynastasija@gmail.com.
2. Go to https://script.google.com and click "New project".
3. Name it "By Nastasija bookings" (top left).
4. Delete everything in the editor, then paste in the whole of Code.gs.
5. Near the top, in SETTINGS, fill in BANK (account name, bank, IBAN, account number).
   Type her dashboard password in ADMIN_PASSWORD (between the quotes). Use something long.
   Check OPEN, CLOSE, OPEN_DAYS and LUNCH match her hours.
6. Click the save icon.

STEP 2 · Run setup once
1. In the function menu at the top (next to "Debug"), choose "setup" and click "Run".
2. Google asks for permission. Click "Review permissions", pick her account.
   If you see "Google hasn't verified this app", click "Advanced" then
   "Go to By Nastasija bookings (unsafe)". This is normal for your own script.
   Allow access (Sheets, Calendar, sending email).
3. When it finishes, open "Execution log": it shows the link to the new bookings sheet.
   Bookmark it on her phone (useful as a backup; day to day she uses the dashboard).
4. Go back to SETTINGS, delete the password from ADMIN_PASSWORD (leave the quotes: '')
   and save. The password is now stored safely in scrambled form.
   To change her password later: type the new one in ADMIN_PASSWORD, run setup() again,
   then clear it again.

STEP 3 · Put it online
1. Click "Deploy" (top right) > "New deployment".
2. Click the cog next to "Select type" and choose "Web app".
3. Description: Website bookings
   Execute as: Me (skinbynastasija@gmail.com)
   Who has access: Anyone
4. Click "Deploy" and copy the "Web app URL" (it ends in /exec).

STEP 4 · Connect the website
1. Open index.html in a text editor (or GitHub's edit pencil).
2. Search for:   apiUrl: "",
3. Paste the URL between the quotes, for example:
       apiUrl: "https://script.google.com/macros/s/AKfy..../exec",
4. In the same CONFIG, fill in bank: { accountName, bank, iban, accountNumber }
   so the website shows the bank details too.
5. Save and upload index.html to GitHub as before.
(Or send Claude the URL and bank details and it will do this for you.)

STEP 5 · Test
0. Go to the website, scroll to the bottom, click "Studio login" and log in with her password.
1. On the website, click Book now > "No, I've been before". It will say the number
   isn't found (the client list is empty). Book a free consultation instead.
2. Check: a row in Consultations, a light blue event in her calendar, and an email.
3. Now Book now > "No, I've been before" with the same number. It's found.
   Book a treatment. Check the Bookings tab, the yellow calendar event and the email
   with the "Send deposit message on WhatsApp" button.
4. Delete the test rows and events afterwards (set Status to Cancelled first to remove the event).


DAY TO DAY (for Nastasija, all in the Studio dashboard)
- New booking email arrives > tap "Send deposit message on WhatsApp" > Send.
  (Or open the booking in the dashboard and tap "Send deposit message".)
- Deposit received > open the booking > Status "Deposit paid" > Save.
  The calendar event turns green and says CONFIRMED.
- After the appointment > Status "Completed". The client's visit count and history update.
- Cancelled > Status "Cancelled". The calendar event is removed and the time opens up again.
- No show > Status "No show".
- Consultations: open the call > Confirmed, Done or Cancelled.
- Client notes (allergies, skin type, colours used): Clients > open the client > Private notes > Save.
- Time off: Block time tab, or add an event straight into her Google Calendar.
- She stays logged in on her phone for 30 days. "Log out" ends it on that device.
- After 8 wrong passwords, logging in pauses for 15 minutes.

EXISTING CLIENTS
Returning clients are recognised by their mobile number. Clients from before the website
won't be in the list yet, so add them in the dashboard: Clients > Add client
(name, mobile, optional notes). After that they can book as returning clients.

PRIVACY
The website only asks "is this number a client: yes or no". Names and treatment history
are never sent back to the website; they stay in her sheet, calendar and email.

IF YOU CHANGE THE CODE LATER
Deploy > Manage deployments > edit (pencil) > Version: New version > Deploy.
The URL stays the same, so the website doesn't need changing.

FULLY AUTOMATIC WHATSAPP (optional, later)
Right now the deposit message is sent by Nastasija with one tap. Sending it with no tap
at all needs the paid WhatsApp Business Platform (Meta approval, a message template and a
small fee per message). It can be added to this script later if she wants it.
