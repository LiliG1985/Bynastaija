BY NASTASIJA · AUTOMATIC WHATSAPP MESSAGES TO CLIENTS
=====================================================
When this is switched on, clients get these WhatsApp messages from Nastasija's
business number automatically, with no tapping:

  1. Deposit request     straight after they reserve a treatment (with bank details)
  2. Consultation booked straight after they book a free consultation call
  3. Booking confirmed   when she marks the booking "Deposit paid" in her dashboard
  4. Reminder            the day before the appointment (treatments with deposit paid, and consultations)

Until it's switched on, everything keeps working with the one tap buttons.


WHAT IT NEEDS
WhatsApp only allows automatic business messages through the official
WhatsApp Business Platform (run by Meta). That needs:
- A Meta Business account, verified with the UAE trade licence and the owner's Emirates ID
  (verification usually takes a few working days).
- A WhatsApp provider ("Business Solution Provider") to connect the number.
  Choose one that supports COEXISTENCE: this lets Nastasija keep using her normal
  WhatsApp Business app on 052 908 6773, with all her chats, while the website also
  sends messages from the same number. (She must open the app at least every 13 days.)
- The 4 message templates below, approved by Meta (usually within a day).

COST
Meta charges per message. Booking messages are "utility" messages; in the UAE these cost
roughly AED 0.04 to 0.06 each (check the current rate with the provider). For example,
100 bookings a month with about 3 messages each is roughly AED 12 to 18 a month,
plus whatever the provider charges for its plan.


STEP 1 · Pick a provider and connect the number
Pick a Meta partner that offers coexistence and gives you API access. Two set ups work
with this website without changes:
  a) Meta's own Cloud API (through Meta Business Suite / developers.facebook.com):
     you get a PHONE NUMBER ID and a permanent ACCESS TOKEN.
  b) 360dialog: you get an API KEY.
Follow the provider's sign up. It will ask you to scan a QR code with the WhatsApp
Business app on Nastasija's phone to link the number (that's the coexistence step).

STEP 2 · Create the 4 templates
In the provider's dashboard (or WhatsApp Manager), create these templates.
Category: UTILITY. Language: English. Copy the names and text exactly;
{{1}} {{2}} etc. are filled in automatically.

  Name: deposit_request
  Text:
    Hi {{1}}, thank you for temporarily reserving your treatment slot with By Nastasija.

    Treatment: {{2}}
    Date: {{3}}

    Please pay a 50% deposit of {{4}} AED to:
    {{5}}
    Reference: {{6}}

    Your slot is held for 24 hours until the deposit is received. Please reply here with your receipt once paid.
  Sample values: Sara · Lip Blush · Thursday 8 October 2026 at 10:00 · 600 · Account name Nastasija · IBAN AE00 0000 0000 0000 · BN-12AB34

  Name: consultation_booked
  Text:
    Hi {{1}}, thank you for booking your {{2}} with By Nastasija on {{3}}. If you can, reply with a few photos taken in daylight before the call. See you soon, Nastasija
  Sample values: Nina · free video call · Friday 9 October 2026 at 19:00

  Name: booking_confirmed
  Text:
    Hi {{1}}, your deposit has been received and your {{2}} on {{3}} is confirmed. See you soon, Nastasija
  Sample values: Sara · Lip Blush · Thursday 8 October 2026 at 10:00

  Name: appointment_reminder
  Text:
    Hi {{1}}, a friendly reminder of your {{2}} with By Nastasija {{3}}. If you need to change it, please reply here.
  Sample values: Sara · Lip Blush · tomorrow at 10:00

Wait until all 4 show as "Approved".

STEP 3 · Add the details to the booking system
In Google Apps Script, in Code.gs, find WA_API in SETTINGS and fill in:

  Meta Cloud API:
    URL:    'https://graph.facebook.com/v21.0/PHONE_NUMBER_ID/messages'   (put her phone number ID in place of PHONE_NUMBER_ID)
    HEADER: 'Authorization'
    TOKEN:  'Bearer ACCESS_TOKEN'                                          (the word Bearer, a space, then the token)

  360dialog:
    URL:    'https://waba-v2.360dialog.io/messages'
    HEADER: 'D360-API-KEY'
    TOKEN:  'API_KEY'

Then:
1. Save, and run setup() once more (this also switches on the daily reminders at 10:00).
2. Deploy > Manage deployments > edit (pencil) > Version: New version > Deploy.
   The website link stays the same.

STEP 4 · Test
Book a test treatment on the website with your own mobile number. Within seconds you
should get the deposit request on WhatsApp, and Nastasija's email will say
"Deposit message sent to the client automatically".
In the dashboard, mark it Deposit paid: you get the confirmation message.
Then cancel the test booking.

IF A MESSAGE DOESN'T SEND
Nothing breaks: Nastasija's email simply shows the one tap "Send deposit message"
button as before. In Apps Script, "Executions" shows the reason (usually a template
name that doesn't match, a template not approved yet, or an expired token).
